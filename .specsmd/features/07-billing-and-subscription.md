# Billing and Subscription

Subscription belongs to workspace.

Personal workspace uses individual plan.

Organization workspace uses organization plan.

## Plan Limits

Plans include:

- max_projects
- max_members
- monthly_srs_generations
- monthly_ai_diagram_generations
- monthly_manual_diagram_saves
- can_use_manual_drawio
- can_generate_srs
- can_generate_ai_diagrams
- can_export_srs
- can_export_diagrams
